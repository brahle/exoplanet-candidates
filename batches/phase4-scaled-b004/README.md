## Survey-wide false-alarm context

The inverted-null controls left **2/1,500 survivors (0.133%)**, with **0.485%**
as the upper endpoint of the two-sided 95% Wilson interval. The sealed b004 live-run
snapshot has **218/54,494 (0.4000%)** initial survivors. If the controls represent
the survey background, their rate ratio suggests roughly **one third** could be
noise/stellar-variability false alarms **before eclipsing binaries**. The upper
endpoint cannot rule out **most or all** initial survivors being false alarms.

This is not an individual planet probability or a measured contamination fraction
for these 73 later-selected dossiers. Asynchronous live-run counters and the
control-transfer assumption remain explicit.
